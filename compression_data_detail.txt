### Core Control Requirements
SC-7 mandates three specific actions to protect system boundaries:
1.  **Monitor and Control Communications:** Organizations must monitor and control communications at:
    *   External managed interfaces to the system.
    *   Key internal managed interfaces within the system.
2.  **Implement Subnetworks for Public Access:** Publicly accessible system components must be placed in subnetworks that are either **physically** or **logically** separated from internal organizational networks. These separated subnetworks are referred to as **Demilitarized Zones (DMZs)**.
3.  **Managed Interfaces for External Connections:** Connections to external networks or systems must occur **only** through managed interfaces. These interfaces must consist of boundary protection devices arranged in accordance with an organizational security and privacy architecture.

*   Gateways
